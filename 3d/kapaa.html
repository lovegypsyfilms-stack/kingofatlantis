Kapaʻa — catch the bus

Real Kapaʻa streets from OpenStreetMap, and The Kauaʻi Bus on its real timetable along Kūhiō Hwy (400 to Līhuʻe, 500 to Hanalei). You're Ben, outside his place, heading to Līhuʻe — that's the 400, from the stop across the road. Follow the gold arrow — someone's usually already waiting — then "Wait for bus" skips ahead until it's pulling in. When the doors open, board.

On the bus: pick one of the four free seats. Watch the people near you; when something's coming, hold Shield (Space). Do that a couple of times and you'll go into the astral. There: move (WASD / left thumb), strike by tapping where to aim (or F — aim ahead of it), hold Shield when it winds up (that's a read), Roll (R) to dodge. Request a stop to get off.

Guinea pig: he can only get on when someone else does — stay close behind a passenger as they board and tap Sneak on. Then hide under a seat before anyone notices. Space / Jump to hop up onto a seat, a bench — or someone's lap (they'll react). Something else lives under those seats: N to nip. Sneak off when the doors open at a later stop.

Houses on the residential streets are placeholders the map doesn't record yet, placed by the street layout. Hill heights are approximate until we load real elevation data. Ben's place is our pick, not a real address.
