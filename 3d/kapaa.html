Kapaʻa, from the map

The real streets, footpaths, coastal bike path, shoreline and mapped buildings of Kapaʻa and Waipouli, from OpenStreetMap. You start outside Ben's place in old Kapaʻa.

Houses on the residential streets are placeholders the map doesn't record yet, placed by the street layout. Hill heights are approximate until we load real elevation data. Ben's place is our pick, not a real address.
